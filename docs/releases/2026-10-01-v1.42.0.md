# KneadIt v1.42.0

Date: 2026-10-01
Tag: v1.42.0

## Summary

Customers can unsubscribe from marketing email. Customer accounts need a verified email before showing order data. Inventory now converts recipe units and recipes can link ingredients from the admin. Links in scheduled emails point at the bakery's own address, and the referral program rewards on delivery.

## Highlights

### Customers and email
- **Marketing unsubscribe.** Marketing emails (customer campaigns, bulk messages, birthday, repeat-order reminder, abandoned-cart recovery and review request) carry a one-click unsubscribe header and an "Unsubscribe" footer link. Unsubscribed customers are skipped by every marketing sender. The Customers table shows each customer's status, with a filter and a "Mark unsubscribed" action; only the customer can re-subscribe. Order and other transactional emails are unchanged (#1256).
- **Verified email for account access.** A customer account must verify its email before it can open the account dashboard, order history, profile, favorites, or any order page via the account. Registering with an email that already has guest orders keeps the existing name and phone (#1261).
- **Links in scheduled and queued emails** (review requests, cart recovery, campaign open tracking, "back in stock", order status) now use the bakery's storefront address instead of the platform domain, so they open the right site (#1258).
- **Review emails:** each star in the review request links straight to the review form with that rating chosen (#1260).

### Inventory and recipes
- **Unit conversion.** Stock checks at checkout, deduction when baking starts, restock on cancel and the shopping list convert recipe amounts into each ingredient's stock unit (mass, volume and count units). Lines whose units can't be converted (e.g. cups against pounds) are skipped with a warning instead of blocking orders. The recipe form only offers units that match the ingredient (#1255).
- **Linked ingredients save.** The recipe form's "Linked Inventory Ingredients" now saves, edits and removes lines; it previously failed. Stock quantities keep 4 decimal places so small amounts aren't rounded away (#1259).

### Referrals and onboarding
- **Referral rewards on delivery.** Only customers with no earlier orders get the referral discount. The referral stays pending until the referred order is delivered, when the referrer's reward coupon is created (once). A cancelled order cancels the referral (#1254).
- **Custom domains** can't be set to a platform domain or a domain another bakery uses, and they're normalized before saving (#1260).
- **Onboarding subdomains** are lowercased, must be valid hostnames, and can't collide with an existing bakery or a reserved name (#1253).

### Other
- Release profile docs: the merge-back after a release is a direct push (#1252).

## Database and deployment notes

- **Two tenant migrations. Verify tenant backups first, then run tenant migrations:**
  - `2026_10_01_120000_add_marketing_opted_out_at_to_customers_table` adds nullable `customers.marketing_opted_out_at`.
  - `2026_10_01_130000_widen_inventory_quantity_columns` widens `ingredients.current_stock`, `ingredients.low_stock_threshold`, `recipe_ingredients.quantity` and `stock_adjustments.quantity` to 4 decimal places. On SQLite this rebuilds those three tables. Checked before release: no CHECK constraints are lost, foreign keys and indexes are kept, and rows survive with foreign keys enforced.
- **Behavior changes bakers may notice:**
  - Abandoned-cart recovery emails only go to carts whose email matches an existing customer.
  - Bulk messages skip customers who unsubscribed.
  - Customers who registered but never verified their email must verify before seeing their orders.
  - Recipe lines with units that can't be converted no longer count toward stock checks; check recipe pages for the warning hint.
- Follow the standard deployment checklist in [Operations](../operations.md#deployment-and-release), including `bin/sync-version` before configuration caching.
