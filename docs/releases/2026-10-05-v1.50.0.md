# KneadIt v1.50.0

Date: 2026-10-05
Tag: v1.50.0

## Summary

New owners now set up their bakery first and choose a plan later, and three problems found in a production signup test are fixed.

## Highlights

- **Bakery first, plan later** (#1328). Registering now goes straight to bakery setup; the plans page is no longer a step in signup. The bakery's free trial already starts when it's created, and owners choose a plan later through **Manage billing** or the trial emails.
  - Choosing a plan during the trial starts the Stripe subscription with its trial ending on the bakery's own trial end date, so the first charge comes when the free trial ends. Cashier keeps at least 48 hours for a trial that's nearly over. After the trial has ended, billing starts straight away.
  - The plans page shows "Your free trial ends on …" during the trial and no longer says "No credit card required" for checkout; a card is only needed when choosing a plan.
- **New bakery email to the platform** (#1329). The "new bakery signed up" email to platform admins failed every time in the queue. It now builds its admin link from the app URL, and all platform emails render without reading a bakery's settings.
- **Activity log during setup** (#1329). Changes made while a bakery was being created were dropped from its activity log. They're now kept, with the person's name.
- **First product** (#1329). A new bakery has no categories, and the Products form couldn't create one. The category field now has a "create category" option (as the setup wizard already did) and a hint when none exist.

## Database and deployment notes

- No database migrations and no configuration changes.
- Follow the standard deployment checklist in [Operations](../operations.md#deployment-and-release), including `bin/sync-version` before configuration caching.
- After deploying: register a test account and check it goes straight to bakery setup; check the platform "new bakery" email arrives.
