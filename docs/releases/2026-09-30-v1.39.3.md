# KneadIt v1.39.3

Date: 2026-09-30
Tag: v1.39.3

## Summary

Hotfix: online gift card purchases are turned off until a paid checkout is available, and the gift card balance check works again.

## Changes

- **Online gift card purchases are turned off.** The Gift Cards page no longer has a purchase form. It now says gift cards are available at the bakery. Staff can still issue gift cards from the admin panel. A paid online checkout will come in a later release. The `POST /gift-cards/purchase` endpoint is removed.
- **Balance check fixed.** The page's balance checker misread the server's response and always showed "Gift card not found." It now shows the balance.

## Page content

The Gift Cards tab under Settings → Page Content now has **In-Store Purchase** texts (eyebrow, heading and description) in place of the purchase-form and success texts. These old keys are no longer used: `amount_label`, `details_heading`, `recipient_label`, `success_heading`, `success_description` and `flash_purchased`.

## Database and deployment notes

- No database migrations.
- After deploying, review gift cards created through the online form, and deactivate any that don't match a sale in the bakery's records.
- Follow the standard deployment checklist in [Operations](../operations.md#deployment-and-release), including `bin/sync-version` before configuration caching.
