# KneadIt v1.57.0

Date: 2026-10-08
Tag: v1.57.0

## Summary

Phone numbers format as they're typed, with a country picker, and are stored in one standard format.

## Highlights

- **Phone input** (#1382). Every phone field in the bakery admin (setup, settings, customers, Quick Order, suppliers, catering, waitlist) and on the storefront (checkout, pickup contact, account register and profile, catering) has a country picker that starts on the US and formats as you type, for example (913) 387-7359. Numbers are validated for the chosen country and stored in E.164 (`+19133877359`).
- **Phone display.** Admin tables and pages, invoices, the printable menu, the storefront footer and contact page, the order confirmation, the account dashboard and emails show numbers in national format for the bakery's country and international format otherwise; `tel:` links use E.164. PayPal invoices send the real calling code.

## Database and deployment notes

- **Migration:** 1 per bakery, `normalize_phone_numbers_to_e164`. It converts phone values on customers, suppliers, waitlist entries, catering inquiries, the order pickup contact phone and the `store_phone` setting to E.164, reading numbers without a country code as US. Values it can't parse are left unchanged; nothing is deleted; running it twice changes nothing.
- **Before deploying:** confirm a fresh central and tenant backup exists (the backup gate).
- **New dependencies:** `propaganistas/laravel-phone` (Composer) and `intl-tel-input` (npm, bundled by Vite; no CDN). The deploy's `composer install` and `npm` build pick them up.
- No new environment variables.
- Follow the standard deployment checklist in [Operations](../operations.md#deployment-and-release), including `bin/sync-version` before configuration caching.
- After deploying: `/up`, a bakery's Settings store phone, the storefront checkout phone field, and Nightwatch on the new release.
