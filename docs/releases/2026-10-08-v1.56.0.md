# KneadIt v1.56.0

Date: 2026-10-08
Tag: v1.56.0

## Summary

A round of fixes across reviews, emails, orders, backups and platform billing, plus a full browser test of bakery setup.

## Highlights

- **Reviews** (#1378). One review per order is enforced in the database, ratings are kept to 1–5, and a low rating alerts the bakery only once even after edits.
- **Bakery setup** (#1375). The logo uploaded during setup is now stored where the admin and storefront can show it. A browser test walks a bakery owner through all 10 setup steps on every release.
- **Emails** (#1373). Plain-text emails show apostrophes and ampersands as typed. Staff invitations and the weekly digest use the KneadIt email layout. Pickup orders get a "Picked Up" subject instead of "Delivered".
- **Bakery admin** (#1371, #1377, #1379). Blocked deletes say why (a customer with orders, an ingredient used in recipes or with stock history). Social post and campaign times use the bakery's timezone. The shopping list ranks priced suppliers first. Quick Order line totals have room for amounts over a thousand.
- **Storefront order page** (#1372). Livewire's inline styles carry the page's CSP nonce. Saved-cart and reorder quantities are capped at 100. Pickup/delivery choices use proper labels.
- **Platform** (#1374, #1380). The platform admin has a favicon; the hidden maintenance preview route is removed. Trial reminders no longer go to paying owners whose card failed. A new daily check (08:00 UTC) emails platform admins account ids that pay but have no bakery.
- **Backups** (#1376). Half-finished backup folders are ignored by retention and the Backups page, and cleaned up after 3 hours.
- **Tests** (#1370). Browser smoke navigations wait long enough for the page to load.

## Database and deployment notes

- **Migrations:** 3 per bakery on `reviews`:
  - a unique index on `order_id` (older duplicate reviews of one order are unlinked from it, never deleted);
  - the 1–5 rating check (out-of-range ratings are clamped first; the table is rebuilt with all columns, indexes and foreign keys kept);
  - a nullable `low_rating_alerted_at` column.
- **Before deploying:** confirm a fresh central and tenant backup exists (the backup gate).
- New scheduled command `platform:check-subscribers-without-bakery` (daily 08:00 UTC); the existing scheduler runs it. No new environment variables.
- Follow the standard deployment checklist in [Operations](../operations.md#deployment-and-release), including `bin/sync-version` before configuration caching.
- After deploying: `/up`, both admin logins, a bakery's Reviews page, and Nightwatch on the new release.
