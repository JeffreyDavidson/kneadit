# KneadIt v1.53.0

Date: 2026-10-07
Tag: v1.53.0

## Summary

Fixes from a full application audit: safer checkout and payments, correct discounts and gift cards, protected order history, reliable database transactions and backups, overdue-payer billing, and a long list of admin and storefront fixes.

## Highlights

- **Checkout and payments** (#1354, #1358, #1361, #1364)
  - Card customers reach Stripe reliably; no false "error submitting your order" and no duplicate orders. Storefront and catering payments are cards only.
  - A payment that arrives after an order was cancelled or paid another way is not applied; the bakery is told to refund it. Cancelling an order closes its Stripe page and cancels its PayPal invoice.
  - "Pay now" on the order page for unpaid card orders.
  - Gift cards no longer pay the tip or get over-drawn; coupons and the sitewide sale no longer stack (the larger wins); up to 100 of one item.
  - Reorders use today's prices and say which discontinued items were left out. The checkout summary keeps the coupon discount right as the cart changes.
  - PayPal uses each bakery's own account and sandbox setting.
- **Order history protected** (#1353). Products and categories that have past orders can't be deleted (deactivate them instead); past orders keep their items and names.
- **Database reliability** (#1352). SQLite transactions take the write lock up front, so checkouts no longer fail with "database is locked". Backups are consistent snapshots checked for corruption, and the health check alerts if the newest backup is over 13 hours old.
- **Platform billing** (#1355). A paying bakery whose card fails gets 7 days before it is paused and is never told its "trial expired". No second subscription while one is unsettled. Free forever means Pro features. Extending a trial unpauses the bakery. The "Account Active" switch is removed (Pause is the one switch).
- **Security hardening** (#1357). Central-only pages answer only on the main site; new bakery users default to Staff.
- **Storefront** (#1359, #1362). The Rewards page requires sign-in. One review per order; uploaded photos have location data removed. Modify Order errors are visible. Labelled form fields, per-page titles and a bakery-branded 404.
- **Admin** (#1356, #1360). Crash and display fixes (customer photos, Holiday announcements, bakery rename, styled error pages, negative money, Quick Order). Tax export includes the last day; supplier prices stored in cents; campaign and blog times in the bakery's timezone; same-day pickup hides past times; correct "Delivered/Picked up" email.
- **Hidden until finished** (#1359): maintenance mode, platform announcements, feature usage, platform-events tab, gift card presets, referral "free month".

## Database and deployment notes

- **Migrations:** 1 central (announcement types) and 4 per bakery: user role default, `order_items.product_id` → set null on delete (with a name snapshot), `products.category_id` → restrict, and missing indexes. The per-bakery migrations rebuild `order_items` and `products`; verified row-for-row on a full demo copy.
- **Before deploying:** confirm a fresh central and tenant backup exists (the backup gate).
- `config/database.php`: SQLite `transaction_mode` IMMEDIATE. `config/services.php`: unused `paypal.sandbox` key removed. No new environment variables.
- The deploy script now ends with a Nightwatch agent restart (#1363).
- Follow the standard deployment checklist in [Operations](../operations.md#deployment-and-release), including `bin/sync-version` before configuration caching.
- After deploying: `/up`, a storefront order page, both admin logins, Products delete refusal on a product with orders, and Nightwatch on the new release.
