# KneadIt v1.48.0

Date: 2026-10-05
Tag: v1.48.0

## Summary

A security, money and reliability release from the second October audit and a review of production errors. It locks down payment credentials and refunds, fixes cross-bakery and script-injection problems, makes every report use one definition of revenue, stops scheduled jobs failing on "database is locked", and adds health checks for the failures production actually had.

It also includes the driver page sign-in fix (#1308), which was released to production as a hotfix earlier today.

## Highlights

### Security and privacy

- **Driver page** (#1308, already live). The driver page listed the day's deliveries, with customer names and addresses, without signing in. It now needs a staff sign-in.
- **Payment credentials** (#1313). The hidden raw settings page that let managers change any stored setting, including where card payments go, is removed. Stripe, PayPal and the payment method choice are owner-only, and the PayPal secret is never sent to the browser; it can only be replaced. Starting Stripe Connect requires the owner.
- **Platform messages** (#1310). A bakery's Messages page could open another bakery's platform messages. It now shows only the bakery's own, platform replies are labelled "KneadIt Team", replies work again, and Staff can't open the page.
- **Script injection** (#1312, #1316). Bakery names on the central directory page are no longer placed inside script, brand colours must be hex codes and are checked everywhere they're shown, and customer names in staff notifications are escaped.
- **Personal data** (#1320). The activity log no longer stores password hashes, remember tokens or other secrets, and entries older than a year are pruned. Managers can anonymise a customer: orders are kept for accounting, and the customer's contact details, notes, favourites, carts, waitlists, photos and survey answers are removed or blanked. Cart recovery emails only go to signed-in or verified customers, and greet them by their stored name.
- **Admin panels** (#1322) send frame, content-type, referrer and permissions headers. Expense receipts are stored privately per bakery and served only to managers on a plan that includes expenses.

### Money

- **Order money fields** (#1314). Subtotal, discounts, gift card amount, delivery fee, tip and total can't be edited after an order is created; new admin orders compute their total on the server. Cancelling an order puts back exactly the gift card amount that was redeemed.
- **Refunds** (#1314, #1317). Cancelling a paid card order refunds it first, from the order page as well as the table. Staff can cancel unpaid orders; refunds and cancelling paid orders need a manager. A Refund action covers orders that were cancelled while still paid. A double click or two tabs can't refund twice: each refund is claimed first, sent to Stripe with a per-order idempotency key, and recorded afterwards.
- **Revenue** (#1315, #1319). Every revenue figure now means paid orders that weren't cancelled or refunded, dated by delivery date in the bakery's timezone: dashboard, Sales, Financial, Goal tracker, weekly digest, tax export, customer spend and lifetime value, product report and admin overview. Average order value was shown 100 times too high; it's fixed. Platform MRR and ARPU count only valid, paid subscriptions (not trials, free-forever, demo or paused bakeries). CSV exports neutralise formula-like values.

### Operations

- **Onboarding** (#1311). The onboarding page couldn't render, which also blocked any bakery that hadn't finished onboarding from its admin. It renders again, imported bakeries are marked as set up, the Stripe status panel works, and the logo upload saves.
- **Production** (#1321). Starting to bake no longer fails when an ingredient would go below zero; the shortfall is shown and flagged as negative stock. Cancelling an order restocks exactly what it used. The driver page lists only orders that are ready, uses the bakery's date, and handles an invalid "delivered" gracefully. An order can't be marked delivered twice. Recording stock usage larger than what's on hand shows a clear message.
- **Content** (#1322). Blog posts show their formatting (sanitised) instead of raw tags, and both RSS feeds are valid.
- **Follow-ups** (#1309). Resetting settings to defaults keeps integrations; Stripe Connect webhooks must come from the bakery's own account; expired single-use tokens are pruned; bakeries that use their own website no longer look stuck in onboarding; health alerts don't depend on the database.

### Reliability and monitoring (#1318)

- Scheduled jobs that read bakeries and then wrote to the central database could fail with "database is locked" when other jobs wrote at the same minute. They now load what they need first.
- Every scheduled task was shown as failed in Platform Operations; the real result of background tasks is now recorded.
- Health checks now cover disk space (below 20% or 5 GB), Redis, the scheduler and the Nightwatch agent.
- Sentry events now carry the deployed release.

## Database and deployment notes

- **Three tenant migrations, all added in place (no table rebuilds):**
  - `orders.refund_claimed_at` (nullable timestamp)
  - `carts.customer_id` (nullable, indexed)
  - `stock_adjustments.order_id` (nullable), backfilled for existing rows from the order number in their notes
- No central migrations. The deploy script runs `php artisan migrate --force` and `php artisan tenants:migrate --force`.
- **After deploy, run once:** `php artisan activity-log:redact-secrets` to redact secrets already stored in the activity log.
- **New scheduled jobs:** `platform:prune-expired-tokens` (daily 04:30) and `activity-log:prune` (daily 04:45).
- **New health checks** may alert on their first run if Redis or the Nightwatch agent isn't reachable.
- Follow the standard deployment checklist in [Operations](../operations.md#deployment-and-release), including `bin/sync-version` before configuration caching.
