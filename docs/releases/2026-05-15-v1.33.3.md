## Summary

Restored production resource article detail pages after live 500 errors on `/resources/{slug}`.

## What Changed

- Renamed the central resource route binding from `{post}` to `{centralPost}`.
- Avoided a collision with tenant post binding that caused resource detail requests to resolve through the wrong route parameter.
- Preserved the public `blog.show` route behavior while making central resource resolution explicit.
