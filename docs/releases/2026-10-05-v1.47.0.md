# KneadIt v1.47.0

Date: 2026-10-05
Tag: v1.47.0

## Summary

Bakery owners can now reach billing from their bakery admin, and a bakery whose trial ends unpaid is properly paused. This release also fixes catering deposits, scheduled campaigns that never sent, order webhooks that were dropped, and a set of security, coupon and email problems found in an audit.

## Highlights

### Billing

- **Manage billing** (#1306). Owners couldn't reach the central billing pages: onboarding signs them out of the central site, and the only central login is the platform admin panel. The bakery admin's Upgrade page now has a **Manage billing** button for the owner. It signs the owner into central billing with a single-use link that expires in two minutes, and lands on the plans page with a link back to the bakery.
  - Trial reminder, trial expired and payment failed emails link to the bakery admin's billing page.
  - Free-forever owners see their complimentary plan and can't start a paid subscription.
  - Billing pages answer 404 on a bakery's own address instead of an error, and the Stripe billing portal returns to the bakery admin.
- **Paused bakeries** (#1304). A bakery whose trial ends unpaid is now marked paused, separately from whether it uses a KneadIt storefront.
  - A paused bakery's storefront shows a paused page, its API refuses requests, and its customers get no automated emails. The owner still gets their own alerts and can still use the admin panel to subscribe.
  - Bakeries that use their own website are now paused too; before, they kept the platform after their trial.
  - A new or reactivated subscription resumes the bakery. Platform admins have Pause and Resume actions.
  - When a subscription ends, the bakery's plan goes back to Starter.
- **Subscription state** (#1300). An ended subscription no longer counts as the current plan, so a returning owner can subscribe again. A second subscription can't be started while one is active, and the free trial is only for first-time subscribers. The central Promo Codes page works with its optional fields left blank. The Stripe billing webhook refuses requests if its signing secret isn't configured.

### Money and security

- **Catering deposits** (#1295). Clicking the deposit link again reuses the open payment page instead of starting a second one, and a payment made on an older page is still recorded. Quotes that are cancelled or completed no longer accept deposits. A payment that can't be applied is flagged to the bakery's owners for a refund. The deposit success and cancel pages are signed links.
- **Birthday coupons** (#1298). Codes are random, one per customer per year.
- **Bakery-local expiry dates** (#1298). Gift cards work through the end of their expiry day in the bakery's timezone. Coupon start and end times are entered in the bakery's timezone.
- **Order webhooks** (#1294). While one order webhook was waiting to be sent, every other order's webhook of the same kind was dropped. Each order now gets its own. Several listeners were also registered twice; each now runs once. Webhook addresses are checked against more private address ranges.
- **Webhook access** (#1302). Only the bakery's owner can see and change webhook settings and delivery history, since webhooks carry customer names and emails.
- **Public API** (#1296, #1302). Inactive products no longer appear through `?include=`, and `/api/menu` lists its products. Bad dates and incomplete invitations return validation errors instead of server errors. The `POST /api/reviews` endpoint is removed, because it accepted reviews without an order. Reviews come from the signed per-order link.
- **Owners** (#1296). Demoting the last owner of a bakery is refused, including when two owners act at the same moment.
- **Sign-in** (#1301). A customer password reset signs out the customer's other sessions. The central forgot-password page gives the same reply whether or not an account exists. Staff email addresses are matched regardless of letter case.

### Emails

- **Scheduled campaigns** (#1299). Scheduled bakery campaigns never sent, and platform campaigns had no scheduled sender. Both now run every 15 minutes. A campaign that is retried after a failure emails only the recipients it hadn't reached.
- **Engagement emails** (#1297, #1305).
  - Cart recovery skips customers who already ordered, even on another device, and only covers carts from the last seven days.
  - Reorder reminders skip customers with an open order.
  - Review requests cover orders delivered within the review delay plus seven days, with one request per customer at a time.
  - Customers born on February 29 get their birthday email on February 28 in other years.
  - The weekly digest uses the bakery's own week.
  - Missed check-in days are caught up.
  - Health alerts are sent directly, without the queue, at most once every six hours per check, with a note when the check recovers.
- **Loyalty points on refunds** (#1305). A full refund takes back the points the order earned, and an order refunded before delivery earns none. Loyalty tiers count points net of refunds.

## Database and deployment notes

- **Five migrations, all applied in place (no table rebuilds):**
  - Central: a unique index on `email_campaign_logs` (campaign and email; duplicates, if any, are reduced to one row and the count is logged), the `tenants.paused_at` column, and the new `billing_handoff_tokens` table.
  - Tenant: `customer_id` and `birthday_year` on `coupons` with a unique index, and a unique index on `customer_campaign_logs` (campaign and email, with the same duplicate handling).
- The deploy script runs `php artisan migrate --force` then `php artisan tenants:migrate --force`, so both sets apply on deploy.
- **Paused state:** bakeries paused before this release (storefront turned off after a trial) aren't marked paused. Production has none.
- **Billing:** owners now reach billing through **Manage billing** in their bakery admin. The bakery must have a linked owner (`tenants.user_id`).
- **Scheduler:** two new scheduled commands, `campaigns:send-scheduled` and `platform:send-scheduled-campaigns`, every 15 minutes.
- Follow the standard deployment checklist in [Operations](../operations.md#deployment-and-release), including `bin/sync-version` before configuration caching.
