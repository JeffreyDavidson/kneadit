# KneadIt v1.41.0

Date: 2026-10-01
Tag: v1.41.0

## Summary

A follow-up audit release: customers must prove ownership of their email before seeing or changing an order, order deletion is restricted to protect financial records, and several checkout, admin and reporting bugs are fixed.

## Highlights

- **Order tracking sends a sign-in link.** "Track your order" no longer lists orders for any email typed in. It always shows the same confirmation message and, when the email has orders, emails a signed link that expires after 30 minutes (one email per customer every 5 minutes). Opening the link shows the orders and unlocks the order pages (confirmation, messages, reorder, edit) for that browser session. Logged-in customers still get direct access, and the order number + email verify page is unchanged (#1250).
- **Order deletion is restricted.** Only managers can delete orders, and only Pending or Cancelled orders that are unpaid and have no refunds. Deleting returns the order's coupon use and gift card balance first. Bulk deletes say how many orders were skipped and why. Everything else should be cancelled instead, which keeps the record (#1247).
- **Mark Paid works for partially paid orders.** Catering orders with a paid deposit can now be marked paid from the orders table and the order page (#1246).
- **Clearer checkout error.** When every product in a cart has been deactivated, customers see "Some items in your cart are no longer available" instead of "date fully booked", for the website and the API. Quick Order now reports its errors to the error log (#1249).
- **Reports:** the weekly revenue chart no longer fails when an expense exists that week, and shows the business-use portion of each expense in dollars. Customer acquisition is charted by the bakery's local month (#1245).
- **Docs:** a release profile for the release process (#1248).

## Database and deployment notes

- No database migrations.
- **Order tracking copy:** bakeries that customized the Order Tracking page text keep their saved hero subtitle and button text, which may still read "Look Up". Update them under Settings → Page Content → Order Tracking if needed. A new "link sent" message has a default.
- **Customer-facing change:** customers who track an order now receive an email. Check that outgoing email works for each bakery.
- Follow the standard deployment checklist in [Operations](../operations.md#deployment-and-release), including `bin/sync-version` before configuration caching.
