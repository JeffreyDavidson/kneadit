# KneadIt v1.51.0

Date: 2026-10-06
Tag: v1.51.0

## Summary

New owners now confirm their email before creating a bakery, platform admins can delete bakeries again, and Filament and one build dependency are updated for security advisories.

## Highlights

- **Email verification before bakery setup** (#1332). After registering, a new owner sees a "check your email" page that names the address, with **Resend** and **Use a different email**. Bakery setup needs a verified email.
  - The verification email is KneadIt-branded and sent from the central site; its link is signed and expires.
  - Opening the link sends an owner with no bakery to bakery setup (the bakery name entered at signup carries over), and an owner who already has a bakery to its admin.
  - Only creating a bakery is gated. Billing, the billing handoff, the bakery admin and existing bakery owners are unaffected.
- **Deleting bakeries** (#1331). The central admin's bulk delete refused every bakery because no permission rules existed for bakeries. Platform admins can now view, create, edit and delete bakeries. A bakery whose owner still has an active subscription is refused with "Cancel this bakery's subscription first."
- **Dependency updates** (#1333). Filament 5.7.8 → 5.8.4 for an advisory about its app-based two-factor settings (a feature KneadIt doesn't use), and `source-map-js` 1.2.1 → 1.2.2, a build-only dependency.

## Database and deployment notes

- No database migrations and no configuration changes.
- Updated published Filament assets ship in `public/js/filament` and `public/css/filament`.
- Follow the standard deployment checklist in [Operations](../operations.md#deployment-and-release), including `bin/sync-version` before configuration caching.
- After deploying: register a test account and check it lands on the "check your email" page, the verification email arrives, and its link leads to bakery setup.
