# KneadIt v1.43.0

Date: 2026-10-01
Tag: v1.43.0

## Summary

Customer emails are matched regardless of letter case. Bulk messages separate order updates from promotions. Custom domains are used in links only once their DNS is verified. Product labels order ingredients by real weight. Platform admin tools for custom domains and impersonation are tightened.

## Highlights

### Customers and messages
- **Email case:** customer emails are stored lowercase and matched regardless of case at checkout, login, password reset, registration, referrals, order tracking, favorites, waitlists and more. `Bob@x.com` and `bob@x.com` are now the same customer. The admin Customers form no longer errors when an email differs only by case (#1263).
- **Bulk messages:** "Send message" asks for a purpose.
  - **Order update** goes to selected customers with an open order, including those who unsubscribed from marketing, as a transactional email.
  - **Promotion** goes only to subscribed customers, with an unsubscribe link.
  - The result says how many were skipped and why (#1266).

### Custom domains
- **Links use a custom domain only once its DNS is verified.**
  - Verification is recorded by the Verify DNS button and when the domain is saved, and a daily check (`tenants:verify-custom-domains`, 05:30 UTC) re-checks it.
  - Until a domain is verified, links use the bakery's subdomain (#1267).
- **Platform admin:** a bakery's custom domain is changed only through Set, Remove and Verify DNS actions on its edit page, with the same validation as the bakery's own Settings page (#1270).

### Labels
- **Printed product labels** list ingredients by weight in grams, heaviest first. Volume and count ingredients (cups, each) come after the weighed ones, and the admin shows a note when ordering is approximate (#1265).
- **The Label Generator** uses the same ordering and adds a "Contains:" allergen line on medium and large labels. It also no longer errors on recipes whose ingredients are stored as rows (#1269).

### Platform
- **Impersonation tokens** only work on the bakery they were issued for, and each can be used once even under simultaneous requests (#1268).

## Database and deployment notes

- **Two migrations. Verify central and tenant backups first:**
  - Central: `2026_10_01_120000_add_custom_domain_verified_at_to_tenants_table` adds a nullable column to `tenants` in place (no table rebuild; checked on SQLite).
  - Tenant: `2026_10_01_140000_normalize_customer_email_case` lowercases and trims customer email columns.
    - Customers whose emails differ only by case are **left unchanged and logged** as warnings (tenant id, customer ids, emails), so they can be merged by hand.
    - Colliding favorites are handled the same way.
    - It's safe to run twice.
- **After migrating, run once:**
  ```bash
  php artisan tenants:verify-custom-domains
  ```
  Until then, bakeries with an existing custom domain get subdomain links (which work).
- **Check the logs** after the tenant migration for case-only duplicate customers and merge them. Those customers keep their mixed-case email and won't match lowercase lookups until merged.
- **Behavior change:** bulk "Send message" now requires choosing Order update or Promotion.
- Follow the standard deployment checklist in [Operations](../operations.md#deployment-and-release), including `bin/sync-version` before configuration caching.
