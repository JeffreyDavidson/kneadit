FAQ

Common questions

What is cottage food?
Cottage food refers to certain homemade food products that can be legally made and sold from a home kitchen without a commercial license. Laws vary by state, but most US states allow home bakers to sell baked goods, jams, and other shelf-stable items directly to consumers.
Do I need a license to use KneadIt?
KneadIt is a business management tool — you don't need any special license to use it. However, you should check your state's cottage food laws to make sure you're compliant with local regulations for selling homemade food.
What states are supported?
KneadIt works for bakers in all 50 US states. Our compliance features help you track revenue against your state's annual sales cap, so you always know where you stand.
Can I use my own domain?
Yes! You'll be able to connect your own custom domain to your KneadIt storefront so customers see your brand, not ours.
Is my data secure?
Absolutely. We use industry-standard encryption and security practices to keep your business data and customer information safe. Your data is yours — we'll never sell it or share it with third parties.
