Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about KneadIt.

Cottage food laws allow individuals to prepare and sell certain types of food from their home kitchens without a commercial license. The specific rules vary by state, including what foods are allowed and annual revenue limits. KneadIt helps you stay compliant with your state's regulations.

KneadIt itself doesn't require any license. However, you should check your state's cottage food laws to ensure you're operating legally. Many states just require registration — no formal license. We'll help you understand your state's requirements.

KneadIt works for cottage food bakers in all 50 US states. Our compliance tracking feature knows each state's specific rules, revenue caps, and labeling requirements so you can focus on baking.

KneadIt integrates with PayPal for automatic invoicing. When a customer places an order, they receive a professional invoice they can pay online. You can also track cash and Venmo payments manually. More payment options are coming soon.
